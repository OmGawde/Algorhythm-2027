Technical Council 2026-27 • Key Deliverables

Event Outcomes & Institutional Value

Outcome #01

6-Month Campus Deployment

Selected Hardware / Software Hackathon and Techno-Cultural prototypes addressing VIT operational, venue-booking, network firewall, and smart podium needs are targeted for real campus deployment within 6 months.

Outcome #02

Product Visibility

Provides a high-profile exhibition platform across the Plaza Area andVIT Campus where student-built hardware prototypes, cultural-tech installations, and software products gain live visibility before industry judges, sponsors, faculty, and inter-collegiate visitors—opening doors for patenting, institutional branding, and startup commercialisation.

Outcome #03

Space & UAV Research

Hands-on workshops on ESP32/MPU6050 PID Drone Flight Controllers, OpenCV Computer Vision, CubeSat subsystems, and World Radio Day directly seed student research & startup incubation.

Outcome #04

First-Year Involvement

Actively integrates First-Year (FE) engineering students into core technical culture right from their initial semester through beginner-friendly workshops, intra-college Line Follower Bot challenges, and Ideathon teams—building early hands-on confidence in coding, sensors, and circuit building.

Outcome #05

Identifying & Nurturing Talent to Represent VIT

Identifying and nurturing students from our college to participate in different technical events to represent our college. High-performing teams from intra-college and flagship competitions are mentored further by the Technical Council to represent VIT at IIT/NIT techfests, Smart India Hackathon (SIH), and national robotics championships.

Outcome #06

Placement & Internship Opportunities

Connects standout coders, hardware builders, and problem-solvers directly with industry jury members, corporate sponsors, and tech leads evaluating the Hackathons and exhibitions—creating strong portfolios and direct pathways for technical internships, pre-placement offers (PPOs), and core engineering placements.
